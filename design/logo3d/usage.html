How to use the logo

Two rules and a list of don'ts. The point is that in six months — you, a printer, or whoever edits a thumbnail — nobody has to guess.

1 · Clear space

Keep an empty margin around the logo. Nothing enters it: no text, no photo edge, no button, no page border. The margin is a quarter of the ring's width — that scales automatically, so it works on a business card and on a tarpaulin without doing maths.
X X = ¼ of the ring's width.
Leave X on all four sides.
Eyeball it: the gap should be about as wide as one of the men's heads. If something is closer than that, move it.
