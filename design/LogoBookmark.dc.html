The bookmark

A bookmark is a record of where somebody stopped. That is literally what this app is — not the lesson, but the place each person has reached. The dots are sessions: two done, one to go.
Tradeoff: The dots vanish below about 32px, so the small size has to drop them — you get one shape at homescreen scale and the idea only at large sizes.
