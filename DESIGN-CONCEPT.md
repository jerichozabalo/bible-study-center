## Vocabulary
Jericho's ministry calls a small group a **BGroup (Belong Group)**, not a DGroup — use BGroup everywhere in the app's own copy (decided 2026-08-19).
EXCEPTION: CCF's printed titles keep their own wording — "Book 5: DGroup 101", "Roles of a DGroup Leader", "Handling Issues in DGroups" are the published material's names and are quoted, not relabelled.

## Model
Person (contact fields, home group, joined-at markers) — Group (schedule **incl. time + duration**, current book, archived?) —
Meeting (date, **time (inherited from group, overridable)**, group, book+session optional, **notes (free text, optional)**, proposed/held/cancelled) — Completion (person, session, meeting, attended|present-only)

**Model amendment (2026-08-21, server-first):** `Meeting` gains **`origin`** ('generated' | 'created') per #73 — it exists to scope the idempotency index, and it also answers "did I schedule this or did the app?" for free.

**Model amendment (2026-08-21, audit):** `Completion.session` is **nullable** per #65 — NULL means present-at-a-no-session-meeting. Uniqueness is (person, meeting).

**Model amendments from the calendar session (2026-08-21):** `Group` gains time and duration (#36); `Meeting` gains time (#36) and notes (#55). v1.1 adds `google_event_id` on Meeting (#37/#38). No location entity — #54 puts the venue in notes. Ghosts (#49) are **not rows** — they are drawn from the group's schedule and only become Meetings when tapped.

No new entity for #31: a guest visit is an ordinary Completion whose meeting.group != person.home_group. "Guest" is derived at render time, not stored.
